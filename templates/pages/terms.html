Terms of Service & Buyer Protection

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Buyer Protection & Authenticity Guarantee

Every product listed on QuickCart comes with a 100% authenticity guarantee. Verified sellers undergo KYC document verification before catalog approval. If you receive an incorrect, damaged, or counterfeit product, you are eligible for an immediate 100% refund.

2. 7-Day Hassle-Free Returns

Items purchased on QuickCart can be returned within 7 calendar days of receipt. Products must be in original condition with tags, warranty cards, and packaging intact. Refunds are initiated to the original payment source within 24 hours of item pickup.

3. Payment Security & Data Privacy

QuickCart complies with global PCI-DSS standards. We never store full credit or debit card CVV numbers on our servers. All transaction payloads are encrypted using 256-bit bank-grade SSL protocols.

4. Shipping & Delivery SLA

Standard express deliveries are fulfilled within 2–4 business days. Real-time multi-stage tracking is provided for every order through your customer dashboard.
